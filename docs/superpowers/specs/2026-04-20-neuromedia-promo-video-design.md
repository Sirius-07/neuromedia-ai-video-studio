# NeuroMedia Promo Video Design

## Goal

Create a technology-forward promotional video for NeuroMedia that demonstrates the product workflow in a clear, credible way and ends with a real example video generated by the system.

The first version should be a visual-only cut:

- duration: about 70 seconds
- format: 16:9 landscape
- audio approach: music plus on-screen text, no voiceover in v1
- visual direction: mature AI product film, cool-toned, high-contrast, cinematic UI packaging
- ending: include an existing 10-second output video generated by NeuroMedia

## Audience

This is a general-purpose product promo, not a narrow investor pitch or onboarding tutorial.

It should work for:

- product demos
- landing page embedding
- partner or stakeholder sharing
- social or community distribution

## Core Message

NeuroMedia is not a single-purpose generator. It is a complete AI video creation workflow that moves from idea to script to style to storyboard to editing to final output.

The video should communicate three ideas:

1. The workflow starts from a simple creative input.
2. The system supports control and iteration, not just one-click black-box output.
3. The workflow ends in a real generated result.

## Creative Direction

### Visual Tone

Use a restrained technology aesthetic:

- dark or near-black background treatment when packaging footage
- cyan / blue accent lighting
- crisp UI highlights
- clean typography
- minimal but intentional motion

Avoid:

- loud cyberpunk visuals
- overly glossy template-style promo effects
- excessive transitions that reduce trust in the underlying product

### Motion Language

The footage should feel real, while the Remotion layer adds clarity and momentum.

Preferred motion devices:

- subtle push-ins on important UI regions
- glow or outline highlights around active modules
- masked wipes between product stages
- short light sweeps or flash accents used sparingly
- rhythmic scene timing driven by music beats, but never at the expense of readability

## Production Approach

### Chosen Approach

Use real screen recordings as the base material, then package them in Remotion.

This means:

- actual system interactions remain visible
- long API wait times are compressed rather than shown in real time
- state transitions and emphasis are recreated in Remotion
- the final 10-second generated video is presented as proof of output quality

### Why This Approach

The product has real generation delays because it depends on external APIs and model workflows. A full raw recording would feel slow and unpolished. A fully rebuilt animated promo would look less credible and take longer to produce.

This hybrid approach keeps authenticity while letting the edit remove dead time and add a stronger product-film presentation layer.

## Video Structure

Target runtime: approximately 70 seconds.

### Scene 1: Hook

- timing: 0s to 6s
- content: rapid close-up fragments of the system UI
- examples: prompt input, storyboard cards, timeline view, generation controls
- text: "从一个想法，开始一支视频。"
- purpose: establish this as a complete creation system, not a single screen

### Scene 2: Project Creation

- timing: 6s to 16s
- content: start page or project creation flow
- action: user enters a topic and starts generation
- treatment: keep the real typing and click, then push in on the action area
- text: "一句描述，启动创作流程。"
- purpose: show ease of entry

### Scene 3: Script Generation

- timing: 16s to 26s
- content: script generation and structured script output
- action: show start state and finished state, not the full waiting period
- treatment: use Remotion-generated progress and state animation between the two states
- text: "脚本、节奏与结构，同时展开。"
- purpose: show that the system builds narrative structure, not just visuals

### Scene 4: Style and Storyboard Setup

- timing: 26s to 38s
- content: style selection and storyboard generation views
- action: show visual style choices and generated scene cards
- treatment: use highlight frames and selective zooms to emphasize user control
- text: "风格可选，分镜可控。"
- purpose: reinforce controllability

### Scene 5: Storyboard Iteration

- timing: 38s to 52s
- content: scene adjustment, regeneration, and ordering
- action: show a short montage of iteration behaviors
- treatment: compress multiple interactions into a tight rhythm section
- text: "修改、迭代、重组，都在同一条工作流里。"
- purpose: show that refinement is part of the product

### Scene 6: Editing Workspace

- timing: 52s to 60s
- content: editor, timeline, audio, and finishing capabilities
- action: quickly reveal that music, TTS, and editing belong to the same workspace
- treatment: show enough to imply completeness without turning this section into a tutorial
- text: "从生成，到编辑，到输出。"
- purpose: position NeuroMedia as an end-to-end system

### Scene 7: Final Output

- timing: 60s to 70s
- content: play a real 10-second video generated by the system
- treatment: minimal overlay, only a light brand lockup or closing text near the end
- text: "NeuroMedia"
- optional closing line: "让 AI 创作，进入完整工作流。"
- purpose: deliver proof and emotional payoff

## Editing Rules

### Footage Rules

- use real product recordings wherever possible
- never show long idle waits
- cut from "generation started" to "result appeared"
- prefer clean, confident recordings over exhaustive coverage

### Packaging Rules

- each section should communicate one idea only
- subtitles should be short and declarative
- overlays must clarify the product, not hide it
- the UI should stay readable even during motion

### Final Output Rules

- the final generated video should be the longest uninterrupted section
- avoid heavy overlays during the proof segment
- the ending should feel like fulfillment of the workflow promise

## Remotion Implementation Direction

Use Remotion as the editorial and motion packaging layer, not as the primary content source.

Remotion responsibilities:

- sequence the promo into timed scenes
- animate titles and section subtitles
- crop and scale recordings for close-up emphasis
- add highlight boxes, glow treatment, and transitions
- place the final rendered output video into the closing sequence
- synchronize timing with music

Do not use Remotion to fully fake the product UI in v1.

## Assets Needed

### Required

- clean screen recordings for each workflow stage
- one final 10-second generated video
- product name lockup or final title treatment
- one music track that fits a technology promo pace

### Nice to Have

- cursor-hidden or cursor-controlled alternate recordings
- high-resolution app screenshots for freeze-frame moments
- optional logo mark

## Recording Guidance

To make the edit clean, record short focused clips instead of one long end-to-end session.

Recommended recording units:

- project creation clip
- script generation start clip
- script generation result clip
- style selection clip
- storyboard clip
- iteration clip
- editor clip

This avoids dead time and gives the edit cleaner cut points.

## Success Criteria

The first version is successful if:

- viewers can understand the product workflow without narration
- the video feels like a polished product promo, not a raw tutorial
- no part of the video feels stalled by API waiting
- the ending generated clip feels earned by the earlier workflow

## Out of Scope for V1

- voiceover
- multilingual subtitle versions
- alternate aspect ratios such as vertical social cuts
- full brand system exploration
- full UI reconstruction in animation

## Open Implementation Notes

- The exact music track can be chosen during edit once pacing tests are available.
- Subtitle copy can be tuned after first footage review.
- If any recording section feels visually weak, replace it with a shorter composite built from tighter UI crops rather than extending the raw recording.
